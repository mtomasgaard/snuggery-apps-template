About this data

What the picture is

The light on the cells’ faces and the darker cell edges are part of the drawing, not of the data, and the depth is stretched: the caption under the picture says by how much. Explode pulls the model apart to look inside it (); those distances are display, not geology.

The wells’ paths run from the top of the model through the cells the deck completes them in, and their roles come from its schedule. A producer is a solid line, an injector a dashed one, a shut well thin and faint; a well appears once it first opens.

How the data gets here

The model, the seismic and the wells were built once by the folder’s pipeline (an OPM Flow run of the deck on a Linux runner, and the seismic cut from the survey) and travel inside the app; nothing is fetched. The app re-reads config.json, its settings file, whenever it comes back to the screen.

Drag to turn the model, pinch or use the zoom keys to zoom, and drag with two fingers to pan. Tap a cell or a well name for its values; double-tap a cell to fly to it, and double-tap empty space or use Show the whole field to see it all again. On a keyboard, the arrow keys move the track one report date at a time and Page Up and Page Down .

The Section key opens the section under the model. Inline and Crossline choose the survey’s own lines; Along and Across the field’s two lines through the grid; Draw, then a drag across the field, draws your own, and a drag on A or A′ moves that end. Tap a cell in the section for its values. Under More controls, Section chooses what it shows (the seismic, the model or both), the seismic’s gain and colors, and how strongly the cells cover the seismic.

The slider under the section sweeps the line across the field: an inline or a crossline at a time, from Along through the grid’s rows or columns, from Across through the other, with ‹ and › one at a time; a line of your own moves parallel to itself. Drag the pane’s edge (the grip on the line above it, or its side when it stands beside the model) to make the section taller or wider, or double-tap it to go between compact and its largest; the 3D view keeps a strip of 120 px under it, 160 px beside it. On a keyboard, the arrow keys move the edge.

Pinch the section to zoom it about your fingers, drag it with one finger once zoomed in, or double-tap to zoom in where you tap; a mouse wheel or a trackpad zooms about the pointer. The keys beside the section zoom in and out and fit it again; where the section is too short for them, the keyboard still reaches them. The lock key holds the axes on the whole field, so the slider moves the section and never the scale; zoomed or locked, the view changes only by your hand. Unlocked, a zoom holds until the section changes. Under More controls, the grip’s third raise shows all of Cells and view.
