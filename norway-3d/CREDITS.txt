Norway in 3D: credits and licenses

An online app. The map, the trails, the heights and the snow are read live, as pictures, from the
five sites listed in miniapp.json; the cabins, the code, three.js and the font travel inside the
app. Each license below was read at its source on 2026-10-16. The same credits are in the app's
About.

The credit line About prints first:
Map, heights and trails: © Kartverket, CC BY 4.0; snow: seNorge, © NVE and MET Norway, NLOD; names and cabins: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0


MAP
Topografisk norgeskart (WMTS layers topo, topograatone and toporaster), © Kartverket.
Creative Commons BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); fees and access
constraints: none. Read live from https://cache.kartverket.no/v1/wmts/1.0.0/ as 256 px Web Mercator
tiles. Shown as served: composited over the plate where a tile is transparent, and in 3D draped over
the terrain and lit by the sun the app computes.
Kartverket's terms (https://www.kartverket.no/api-og-data/vilkar-for-bruk) ask that its name be shown
wherever its products are used, as "© Kartverket", with a link where possible: the app's About does.

HEIGHTS NEAR THE VIEW
Nasjonal høydemodell, Digital terrengmodell DTM1 (1 m), © Kartverket.
Open data: NLOD 2.0 (https://data.norge.no/nlod/no/2.0/) and Creative Commons BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Read live from the NHM_DTM_25833 ImageServer at https://hoydedata.no/arcgis/rest/services/, which
resamples the model to Web Mercator tiles at zoom 12 to 15 (512 px; 1.14 m a pixel at zoom 15 and
61.5° N) and, through a raster function the app sends with each request, packs each height into the
color of a PNG. Adaptations: resampled by the service; unpacked by the app to the decimeter; where
the model has no data, filled from the heights in the distance.

HEIGHTS IN THE DISTANCE
Terrain Tiles (Terrarium encoding), from the Mapzen terrain project, on the AWS Open Data registry,
https://elevation-tiles-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/terrarium/. Over Norway the source is Kartverket's 10 m
model. The attribution the project requires
(https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md), word for word for the parts
this app shows (Norway, its neighbors and the sea around them):
Mapzen; ArcticDEM terrain data DEM(s) were created from DigitalGlobe, Inc., imagery and funded under
National Science Foundation awards 1043681, 1559691, and 1542736; Europe terrain data produced using
Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union - EU-DEM layers; Global ETOPO1 terrain
data U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration; Norway terrain data © Kartverket; United
States 3DEP (formerly NED) and global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological
Survey.
Adaptations: heights below the sea's surface drawn at the surface.

TRAILS
Turrutebasen (Tur- og friluftsruter), © Kartverket.
Open data, no conditions apply to access and use
(http://inspire.ec.europa.eu/metadata-codelist/ConditionsApplyingToAccessAndUse/noConditionsApply).
Drawn live by Kartverket's WMS, https://wms.geonorge.no/skwms1/wms.friluftsruter2, layers Fotrute,
Enkel_fotrute, Middels_fotrute, Krevende_fotrute and Ekspert_fotrute, in the service's own colors.
Kartverket's own note on quality: "Åpne data. Datafangsten baserer seg stort sett på innmeldinger
fra lag, foreninger og privatpersoner … Kvaliteten på turrutene avhenger av de målemetodene eller
registreringsmetodene som er brukt for den enkelte turrute."

information. Note that real time data is not quality controlled."
Read live from https://kart.nve.no/enterprise/services/seNorgeGrid_png/ImageServer/WMSServer, as
pictures in the service's eight legend classes, read back by the app into how white the ground is
drawn. While the snow is on, the app shows under the map: "Snow depth on <day>: seNorge, modeled
by NVE and MET Norway on a 1 km grid, from www.senorge.no." The seNorge logo is not shown (the
template carries no agency emblem); that is an open question for the owner.

NAMES
© OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database License 1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). data/labels.geojson is a derived database under
the same license: the elements with a name tagged natural=peak, place=city, town or village,
natural=glacier, and natural=water with water=lake or reservoir (bounding box at least 0.3 km²),
inside Norway's border south of 71.5° N, retrieved through the Overpass API on 2026-10-16 (OSM base
2026-10-16T14:22:18Z), reduced to name, kind, ele, population and box area. tools/build_labels.py
rebuilds it. The credit "Names and cabins: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL." stands under the
map while either is on.

CABINS
© OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database License 1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/); see https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.
data/cabins.geojson is a derived database under the same license: the 980 elements tagged
tourism=wilderness_hut or tourism=alpine_hut inside Norway's border, retrieved through the Overpass
API (https://overpass-api.de/) on 2026-10-16 (OSM base 2026-10-16T12:23:05Z), reduced to their name,
kind (dnt:classification, else the tourism tag), operator, network, capacity, ele, ut.no website and
OSM id. tools/build_cabins.py in the template repository rebuilds it. The app shows the credit
"Names and cabins: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL." under the map while the cabins are on, as
OpenStreetMap's attribution guidelines ask.

This is a planning and orientation tool. It has no position fix, no compass and no live weather,
and it is not a navigation aid. Carry a map and a compass, and check the conditions before you go.
