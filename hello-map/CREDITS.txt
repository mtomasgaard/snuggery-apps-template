Hello Map: sources, licenses and credits. The same words are in About, inside the app.

Map: USGS The National Map.

The map is USGS The National Map's topographic base map (the USGSTopo service) and its imagery with
the topographic map over it (the USGSImageryTopo service), shown as served, as 256 px pictures from
basemap.nationalmap.gov. Nothing of it travels inside this folder.

The acknowledgment USGS asks for, word for word:
Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program.

USGS's terms, read at www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-are-terms-uselicensing-map-services-and-data-national-map
on 19 Oct 2026: "Map services and data downloaded from The National Map are free and in the public
domain. There are no restrictions; however, we request that the following acknowledgment statement
of the originating agency be included in products and data derived from our map services when
citing, copying, or reprinting: "Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey,
National Geospatial Program.""

The topographic map service names its sources as the National Boundaries Dataset, 3DEP Elevation
Program, Geographic Names Information System, National Hydrography Dataset, National Land Cover
Database, National Structures Dataset and National Transportation Dataset; USGS Global Ecosystems;
U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line data; USFS Road data; Natural Earth Data; U.S. Department of State HIU;
and NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. The imagery service names The National
Map's orthoimagery and US Topo.

This app is not made or endorsed by the U.S. Geological Survey.

The collar (the ticks, crosses and degree labels), the scale bar and the coordinates are this app's
own, worked out from the view in Web Mercator.
