Norne benchmark case

Source: the Norne field simulation model published by Equinor and the Norne
licence partners through NTNU and the Open Porous Media (OPM) initiative,
https://github.com/OPM/opm-data (folder "norne", deck NORNE_ATW2013.DATA).

Licence: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

This folder is a derived database. Grid geometry, cell connections and static
properties come from the model's EGRID/INIT output. Saturations and pressures
come from running the public deck with OPM Flow 2026.04 (Python bindings) from
6 Nov 1997 to 1 Dec 2006. The only change to the deck was removing RPTSCHED
report settings from the schedule so that restart output is written monthly.

Check against published results: OPM's opm-tests repository carries Eclipse
2014.2 summary results for the same deck (norne/ECL.2014.2). At 1 Dec 2006:
  cumulative oil    -0.2%
  cumulative water  +0.4%
  cumulative gas    -0.1%
  water injected    +0.1%
  average field pressure within 0.1 bar over the run
Published results contain field and well summaries only, no 3D cell data.

Files
  model.json       dimensions, frame dates, wells (path, completions, role per frame), rates (Sm3/day, averages between frames)
  geometry.bin     float32, 8 corners x (x, y, depth) per active cell, metres, relative to model.center
  neighbours.bin   int32, 6 face neighbours per active cell (-I, +I, -J, +J, up, down), -1 = open face
  ijk.bin          uint8, 0-based I, J, K per active cell
  static.bin       float32 blocks of PORO, PERMX, PERMZ, NTG, FIPNUM, DEPTH
  dynamic.bin      per frame: SWAT uint8 (/255), SGAS uint8 (/255), PRESSURE uint16 scaled to dynamic.pressureRange


================================================================================
Topside layers (data/topside.json, data/topside-network.json)
================================================================================

These two files are optional layers drawn above and beyond the reservoir: the
sea surface and seabed, the Norne FPSO and its riser base, the subsea templates,
the gas export line, the transport network, the onshore terminals, the
neighbouring field outlines, a coastline, and the field's reported monthly
production. They are separate files under separate licences, and the licences
are NOT merged with the reservoir's.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Facilities, pipelines, field outlines and reported production
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Contains data under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD)
distributed by the Norwegian Offshore Directorate (Sokkeldirektoratet),
https://factpages.sodir.no/ and https://factmaps.sodir.no/, retrieved
2026-09-22 and 2026-09-23.

  Licence: NLOD 2.0, https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0
  Layers:  FactMaps 307 (all facilities), 311 (pipelines), 502 (field by status)
  Report:  FactPages field_production_monthly (monthly volumes per field)

Changes made, as NLOD section 5 requires them to be clearly indicated:
  - positions re-centred on the simulation model's origin (E 459,130.28 /
    N 7,323,122.19, centre depth 2,764.62 m) and rounded to 1 m;
  - longitude/latitude requested from the same service in EPSG:4326 for the flat
    network panel, and rounded to 4 or 5 decimals;
  - start-up dates converted from the published epoch values to calendar dates
    in Europe/Oslo (reading them as UTC moves every winter date back one day);
  - monthly production volumes divided by the days in each month to give a daily
    rate, and aligned to the viewer's 110 frames;
  - multi-part pipelines rejoined, and detached parts shorter than 5 km that do
    not touch the main line dropped (one of them is a 1.7 km stub at the north
    end of the Asgard Transport trunk);
  - the coastline simplified to about 1 km, and the neighbouring field outlines
    to about 400 m on the flat map and 25 m in the 3D copy;
  - facilities with no published position (two virtual gas sale points) left out.

What is drawn from published geometry and what is not:
  - PUBLISHED: the FPSO, the riser base, the seven templates, the 16-inch gas
    export line (128.1 km), the Asgard Transport trunk (688.1 km from the
    Norne/Heidrun tee to the Kalsto landfall), the other trunk lines, the
    terminals, and the field outlines.
  - NOT PUBLISHED, drawn as schematic and labelled as such in the app itself
    (in the toggle's own row, not only here): the flowlines and risers between
    the templates and the FPSO, the first 500 m of the export line from the
    riser base, the legs from the drawn well paths up to their templates, the
    shape and heading of the FPSO hull, the depth profile of the export line,
    and the oil leg after the FPSO.
    The Directorate states that its pipeline dataset has a detail level of
    +/- 50 m and that "tracks nearer than 500 meter from facilities is not
    included", which is why the in-field flowlines do not exist anywhere.
  - NOT DRAWN AT ALL: any route from Norne to a refinery. Oil leaves Norne by
    shuttle tanker and no open dataset records where a cargo goes. Mongstad and
    Slagentangen appear as terminals on the map with no line to Norne, because
    drawing one would be the misleading presentation NLOD section 6 forbids.

Coordinate frame: the simulation deck names no datum (its MAPAXES is the
identity transform), so the frame the reservoir is drawn in is an assumption.
Every Directorate record for Norne carries the datum ED50, and the service
stores this data natively in EPSG:23032 (ED50 / UTM 32N), so ED50 is what is
assumed here. The difference to WGS84 at Norne is 80 m east and 209 m north,
which is smaller than the 139-861 m scatter between a well and its own template;
no claim drawn in the app depends on it, and the app never says that a well sits
directly under a particular template.

2. Coastline
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Natural Earth, 1:50m physical coastline, version 4.1.0, from
https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/50m/physical/ne_50m_coastline.zip
retrieved 2026-09-22. Made with Natural Earth.

  Licence: public domain. "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map
  data found on this website are in the public domain." No attribution is
  required; this note is here because the change below should be stated.

Changes: clipped to north-west Europe (6 W - 16 E, 48 N - 68 N) and simplified
to a 1 km tolerance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Which well belongs to which template
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

From the simulation deck itself: the WELSPECS group of each of the 36 wells, and
the GRUPTREE parent where that group is one of the deck's two dummy groups. The
deck is the same ODbL-licensed Norne benchmark case described at the top of this
file, at commit eaa2261683a97027e057c2bc49612ad1c86390b3 of
https://github.com/OPM/opm-data (file norne/INCLUDE/BC0407_HIST01122006.SCH).
The manifolds are MANI-B1, MANI-B2, MANI-C, MANI-D1, MANI-D2, MANI-E1, MANI-E2,
MANI-F and MANI-K2, so the assignment is the deck's own and not a guess: a
nearest-template guess would disagree with it for 23 of the 36 wells.

Files
  topside.json          stage 1: sea, seabed, 9 facilities, 10 lines, the
                        well-to-template map, the flow reference rates and the
                        110-frame reported production series
  topside-network.json  stage 2: the trunk lines, terminals, offshore nodes,
                        field outlines and coastline in longitude/latitude for
                        the flat network panel, plus the tie-back satellites and
                        neighbouring outlines in model-relative metres. This
                        version draws only the flat network panel; the satellites
                        and the model-relative outlines are carried in the file
                        and are not drawn anywhere in the app.
