About this data

What the picture is

The model is the Norne field’s reservoir, under the Norwegian Sea, cut into 44 431 active cells. Each cell is colored by its value on the scale under the picture, at the report date in the player. Where cells go past a scale’s end, the end prints open (≤ and ≥) and those cells take the end’s color.

The light on the cells’ faces and the darker cell edges are part of the drawing, not of the data, and the depth is stretched: the caption under the picture says by how much. Explode pulls the model apart to look inside it (); those distances are display, not geology.

The wells’ paths come from the deck’s completions and their roles from its schedule. A producer is a solid line, an injector a dashed one, a shut well thin and faint; a well appears once it first opens.

The section

The cut, on the track

It is the simulation’s field production: the run of the public deck, driven by the field’s historical well controls, averaged over each month. It is not the field’s reported production; against the field’s own history the model lifts about 7 % less oil and 43 % more water. Gas is not in it, because at the surface the field’s gas is measured in volumes about 220 times the oil’s; gas and injection are in the rates chart under More controls.

How the data gets here

The model was built once by the folder’s pipeline on a Linux runner and travels inside the app; nothing is fetched. The app re-reads config.json, its settings file, whenever it comes back to the screen.

Drag to turn the model, pinch or use the zoom keys to zoom, and drag with two fingers to pan. Tap a cell or a well name for its values; double-tap a cell to fly to it, and double-tap empty space or use Show the whole field to see it all again. On a keyboard, the arrow keys move the track a month at a time and Page Up and Page Down a year.

The Section key opens the section under the model. Along and Across choose the field’s two lines; Draw, then a drag across the field, draws your own, and a drag on A or A′ moves that end. Tap a cell in the section for its values.
