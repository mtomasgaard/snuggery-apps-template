Running distance, pace and heart rate use the watch's run/walk detection, so walk breaks inside a session are not counted as running. Heart-rate zone load is computed in this app from time in zone (Edwards TRIMP: minutes in Z1–Z5 weighted 1–5). Garmin's own acute/chronic load is shown separately on the Load pane and only exists from the day Garmin started recording it for your account.
