MALE BODY (data/geometry*.bin, data/anatomy.json)

Geometry: BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS).
- Skin, muscles, organs, brain, cartilage, bones and teeth: release 3.0, licensed under Creative
  Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP); DBCLS now publishes BodyParts3D under
  CC BY 4.0. https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
- Arteries and veins: release 4.0 (isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99), CC BY 4.0, taken from the browser-ready copy
  in the Human-Atlas package by slorksmo (https://github.com/slorksmo/Human-Atlas, MIT code, CC BY 4.0
  data), where they had been simplified with meshoptimizer at 0.2% relative error.
Mitsuhashi N. et al. BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts.
Nucleic Acids Research 37 (2009) D782-D785. doi:10.1093/nar/gkn613
Adaptations: millimetres Z-up converted to metres Y-up, vertices welded; bones simplified with
meshoptimizer (about 0.1% maximum error per structure), soft tissue simplified with quadric
decimation (fast-simplification) to a per-layer triangle budget; the release 4.0 vessels shifted
onto the release 3.0 skeleton and the segments of each named vessel merged into one structure;
positions quantised to 16 bits. The adapted geometry is shared under the same licences.

FEMALE BODY (data/female/)

Female reference anatomy: Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein, Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP,
3D Reference Organ Set for Female v1.10 (2026), with the ischium and pubis of v1.5. CC BY 4.0.
https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library  Built on the Visible Human Dataset of the U.S. National
Library of Medicine (Spitzer V.M., Whitlock D.G., The Anatomical Record 253 (2002) 49-57).
Hip and leg muscles: Andreassen T.E., Hume D.R., Hamilton L.D., Walker K.E., Higinbotham S.E.,
Shelburne K.B. Three Dimensional Lower Extremity Musculoskeletal Geometry of the Visible Human
Female and Male. Scientific Data 10, 34 (2023). doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01905-2. CC BY 4.0.
Borrowed skull, rib, shoulder, arm and foot bones: BodyParts3D 4.0, (c) DBCLS, CC BY 4.0 (above).
All three were assembled, fitted to one body and simplified (meshoptimizer, 0.2% relative error) in
the Human-Atlas package by slorksmo (https://github.com/slorksmo/Human-Atlas, commit 5bb5713); its
ATTRIBUTION.md records every fitting step. Adaptations here: enumerated pieces merged into one
structure, quadric decimation to a per-layer triangle budget, positions quantised to 16 bits.
The adapted geometry in data/female/ is shared under CC BY 4.0.
